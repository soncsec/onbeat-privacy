OnBeat — Support

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Need help with OnBeat, found a bug, or have an idea? Open an issue and we'll get back to you. A free GitHub account is needed to post.

Common questions

My taps feel right but score as early or late. Every speaker and pair of headphones adds a little audio delay. Tap the gear on the home screen and choose Calibrate. It takes about 10 seconds, and the app remembers it for each output.

Do Bluetooth headphones work? Yes. Bluetooth adds more delay, and it can shift a little each time they connect, so the app may suggest a quick recalibration. Wired headphones or the built-in speaker give the tightest timing.

How many tries do I get at the Daily Beat? Three a day. Listening is free: hear the beat as many times as you like, then tap I'm ready. A try is used once the tapping starts.

When does the Daily Beat change? At midnight in your local time zone. Everyone gets the same beat for the same date.

Can I see the beat while I tap? Yes. Tap the gear on the home screen and turn on showing beats while tapping.
