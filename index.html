OnBeat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

OnBeat ("the app") is a rhythm game: listen to a drum beat, then tap it back. It has a Daily Beat and Practice rounds. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we don't collect

The app has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, microphone, camera, or photos. Calibration uses your screen taps, not the microphone.

Stored on your device only

The app keeps a few things on your device so it works the way you expect. They are never sent anywhere:

  • Your Daily Beat tries and scores for each day, your best try, and how many times you listened
  • Your best score for each Practice beat
  • Your calibration for each speaker or pair of headphones (how much audio delay to allow for)
  • Your settings, such as showing beats while tapping

Deleting the app removes them.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

To improve the game, the app sends a few anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service:

EventWhat it includes
A round startedWhich beat (a Practice beat or that day's Daily Beat); how many times in a row you've played it
A round finishedWhich beat; your score and score range; whether you tended to tap early or late; how many extra taps you made; how many times you listened first
You left the results screenWhich beat; your score and score range; whether you tapped Try again; how long you stayed on the results
A round was stoppedWhich beat; why the audio stopped (for example a phone call or headphones unplugged)
Calibration was savedThe kind of audio output (speaker, Bluetooth or other, never its name); the measured delay, rounded; how consistent your taps were

TelemetryDeck also receives standard technical details: app and iOS version, device model, language, and a random identifier that is hashed so it can't be traced back to you. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. The analytics service may keep a small temporary cache of these events on your device until they are sent.

Game Center

Leaderboards are optional and use Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in, the app sends your score from your first try at each day's Daily Beat to Apple, and you can view the leaderboard. Apple handles your Game Center account under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store your Game Center details anywhere else.

Sharing

When you tap Share on your Daily Beat results, the app creates a short text summary (day number, score, perfect beats and listens) and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app to share it with.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
